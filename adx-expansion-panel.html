Expansion Panel Component

A clickable text link with chevron that reveals a content panel. Uses a slot pattern for flexible content.

Left Aligned

This content is revealed when the panel is expanded. You can place any content here including text, forms, images, or other components.

The gray background and rounded corners provide visual containment for the expanded content.

Center Aligned

Center-aligned panels are useful for callouts or when the trigger appears in a centered layout.

Use the --full-width modifier if you need the panel to span beyond the default 500px max-width.
