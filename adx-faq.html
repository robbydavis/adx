Before applying, please gather the following documents:

  • Social Security number
  • Proof of income (pay stubs or tax returns)
  • Bank statements from the past 3 months
  • Information about existing debts or assets

Having these documents ready will help speed up the application process. For more details, visit our application checklist.

Most applications receive a decision within 2-3 business days. Complex applications may require additional review and documentation.

You'll receive email updates throughout the process, and you can check your application status anytime through your online account.
